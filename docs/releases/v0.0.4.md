## English

### Changes

- The sidebar shows remaining percentages and reset times for both five-hour and weekly windows while the current conversation selects an OpenAI Codex model.
- The Codex card does not render after switching to DeepSeek, Grok, or another Provider, so account information is not stacked by default.
- The sidebar reuses the existing strictly validated loopback usage RPC and introduces no new credential, account-identity, or raw-response exposure.

### Compatibility

- DeepSeek Harness: exact `0.1.1-rc.2`.
- Node.js: `>=22.19.0 <25`.
- This release displays quota and reset times only; it offers no top-up or payment entry.
- Full checks and Linux/macOS/Windows `3/3` are bound to the accepted candidate and approved by the maintainer. The protected workflow still verifies the candidate, supply-chain evidence, the Registry, and the GitHub Release. Real-account OAuth, streaming, tools, images, and transport acceptance starts at `0/13` in a fresh `0.0.4` record and does not inherit evidence from an older release.
