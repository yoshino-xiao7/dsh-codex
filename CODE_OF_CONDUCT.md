# 社区行为准则 / Community Code of Conduct

## 中文

我们致力于建立开放、友善、无骚扰的社区，不因经验、身份、背景或观点排斥参与者。

期望行为包括：尊重不同意见；聚焦可验证的技术事实；给出建设性反馈；承认错误并修复；保护报告者、用户和测试账号的隐私。

不可接受行为包括：骚扰、歧视、威胁、公开他人隐私；诱导他人发布 token、OAuth code 或私人数据；在 Issue/PR 中持续进行人身攻击或无关推广；明知来源不明仍提交复制代码。

维护者可以编辑、隐藏或移除违反准则的内容，并根据严重程度警告、临时限制或永久禁止参与。请通过 GitHub 仓库的私下安全报告入口联系维护者；紧急安全报告遵循 [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md)。

本准则参考 [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)。

## English

We are committed to an open, welcoming, harassment-free community regardless of experience, identity, background, or viewpoint.

Expected behavior includes respecting different opinions, focusing on verifiable technical facts, giving constructive feedback, acknowledging and repairing mistakes, and protecting reporter, user, and test-account privacy.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, soliciting tokens/OAuth codes/private data, persistent personal attacks or unrelated promotion in issues and pull requests, and knowingly submitting copied code of unclear origin.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove violating content and may warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently ban participation according to severity. Use the repository's private security-reporting channel to contact maintainers; urgent security reports follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

This policy is informed by [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
